Update your tracking starting point

If you traded outside Lens, use today’s holdings as the new reference. This resets drawdown and deployment tracking, including during a real market decline.

Reset portfolio tracking?

This reads the selected IBKR account and makes its current holdings your new starting point. It clears the previous market-peak reference and cash-deployment steps, even during a real market decline. Use it once your outside trades have settled. Avoid resetting during a real market decline: it erases the reference your rules depend on.
